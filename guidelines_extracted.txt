              Digital Health Day 026 
 
 
1. General Instructions 
• Abstracts must be original and unpublished     
work 
• Must be relevant to Digital Health 
• Submissions are accepted for e-Poster 
Presentation only 
• All abstracts will undergo review by the 
scientific committee and the committee 
decision will be final. 
 
2. Abstract Structure (Mandatory Format) 
• Title: Clear and concise 
• Authors & Affiliation: Full names and 
institution 
• Background / Introduction 
• Objective / Aim 
• Methodology 
• Results / Findings 
• Conclusion 
*Review Papers: objectives, methodology 
(search strategy & selection criteria), and a 
synthesised summary of findings.   
 
3. Formatting Guidelines 
• Word Limit: 250–300 words 
• Language: English 
• Font: Times New Roman, Size 12 
• Line Spacing: 1.5 
• Alignment: Justified 
• No tables, figures, or images 
 
4. Title & Author Details 
• Do not include degrees (Dr. Prof. etc) 
• Author/s Full Name,  
  Institution 
  UG/PG/PhD, and Presenting author 
should be clearly mentioned at the top of 
the abstract page. 
 
5. Submission Process 
• Submit via Email only 
• File format: .doc or .docx 
• File naming: 
Abstract_Name_Institution.docx 
 
6. Important Dates 
• Submission Deadline: 3.05.2026 
• Acceptance Notification: 07.05.2026 
• Final E-Poster Submission: 10.05.2026  
 
7. Important Notes 
• No plagiarism 
• Abstract cannot be modified after 
submission 
• At least one author must register for 
submitting the Final copy of the e-poster  
• Certificates for presenting authors 
only(In case of multiple authors for the 
poster) 
 
8. Review Criteria 
• Relevance to theme 
• Clarity 
• Methodology 
• Innovation 
• Impact

 Guidelines for Submission of Final e - Poster  
Digital Health Day 2026 
1. File Format & Naming 
*Use ppt.to create the poster, then convert as a *PDF 
file for final submission.  
*Submit the e-poster in PDF format only. 
*File name should follow this format: 
EPoster_PresenterName_AbstractID.pdf 
*Ensure the file size does not exceed 10–15 MB 
6. Language & Formatting 
*Use clear and professional 
English. 
*Avoid grammatical and 
spelling errors. 
*Use bullet points instead of 
long paragraphs 
2. Poster Dimensions & Layout 
*Recommended size: 16.9) aspect ratio 1920x1080 
pixels) – (widescreen format) 
*Orientation-Landscape  
*Maintain a clear, readable layout with proper margins. 
*Use a minimum font size of 24 pt for body text. 
*Poster should be a single page only 
6. Originality & Ethics 
*Content must be original and 
not previously published. 
*Properly cite all sources. 
*Ensure patient confidentiality 
3. Content Structure 
*Include the following sections: 
Title of the study 
Author(s) and affiliation(s) 
Background / Introduction 
Objectives 
Methods 
Results (with charts/figures) 
Conclusion 
References (optional) 
The content structure may be adapted based on the type of 
study. However, poster should broadly follow the given 
content structure. 
7. Submission Process 
*Submit via the designated 
email. 
*Include: Presenting Author’s 
name, Abstract ID, Registration 
ID and contact details. 
*Adhere strictly to the 
submission deadline. 
 
4. Visual Design 
*Use high-resolution images and graphs (minimum 300 
dpi). 
*Maintain consistent colour schemes and fonts, and use 
high contrast colours 
*Use minimal text and more visuals (charts, diagrams, 
icons etc) 
*Provide captions for all images. 
8. Review & Acceptance 
*Posters will be reviewed by the 
Scientific Committee 
*Authors will be notified 
regarding acceptance or 
required modifications 
5.Font size and Readability 
*Title : 60-100 pt 
*Section Headings: 36-50pt 
*Body text: Minimum 28-36pt 
*Content should be readable from a distance of 2-3 
meters 
 
9. Presentation (if applicable) 
*Presenters should be available 
during the poster session. 
*A short oral explanation (3-5 
minutes) will be required. 
*Be prepared to answer 
questions from the judges 
 
Submit your final e-poster to:digihealth.kaher@gmail.com 
Last Date of Submission: 10.05.2026
